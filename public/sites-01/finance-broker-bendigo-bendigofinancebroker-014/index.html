Equipment, Asset And Cash Flow Paths

The published service list separates equipment finance, asset finance, commercial vehicle finance, machinery finance, business loans and working capital solutions. Rather than treating these as labels, compare them by asking what the lender is securing, how repayments are structured, and whether the facility is meant to buy a productive asset or cover operating needs.

  • Equipment and machinery: ask whether chattel mortgage, finance lease or hire purchase is available for the item, and whether seasonal payments are relevant for farm machinery.
  • Commercial vehicles: ask about new and used trucks, utes, vans and trailers, and whether the term and balloon payment suit the asset life.
  • Working capital: ask whether the request is better framed as cash flow support, debtor finance or an overdraft alternative.
  • Business loans: ask whether secured or unsecured funding is being compared, and what documents are needed for the amount requested.

Questions To Ask Before Comparing Rates

Rate comparison is only one part of a commercial finance decision. ASIC Moneysmart encourages borrowers to understand loan features, fees and repayment obligations before committing to a loan. For business finance, the practical version is to ask how the structure behaves if cash flow is uneven, if the asset is upgraded, or if the business wants to pay out the loan early.

Use the quote stage to ask for the total repayment picture, any upfront or ongoing fees, the treatment of GST where relevant, the balloon amount if one is proposed, and whether additional security is required. The source page says the equipment itself usually serves as security for most equipment finance, while unsecured options are also available.

Tax language should be handled carefully. The provider notes that chattel mortgages can allow GST upfront and depreciation deductions, while finance leases charge GST on repayments. The page also recommends discussing the best option with an accountant, which is the right checkpoint before choosing a structure for tax reasons.

Documents And Approval Expectations

The provider states that pre approval is often available within 24 hours for standard applications, while full approval usually takes 2 to 5 business days depending on complexity and documentation completeness. Those timeframes should be treated as application dependent, because missing financials, unclear asset details or a complex business structure can slow assessment.

Before requesting a quote, prepare the asset invoice or description, business identification, trading history, income details and the reason for the finance. For working capital, be ready to explain whether the need is seasonal, linked to debtor timing, or tied to expansion. For machinery and vehicle finance, be specific about the item, age, use and expected income contribution.

A broker comparison is strongest when each lender is assessing the same brief. Keep the requested amount, term preference, balloon question and settlement deadline consistent, then compare the repayment, fees, security and flexibility side by side.

  1. Define the purpose. Write down the asset, loan amount or cash flow need before asking for lender options.
  2. Choose the structure questions. Ask whether chattel mortgage, finance lease, hire purchase, secured loan or unsecured funding best fits the purpose.
  3. Prepare evidence. Gather asset details, business trading information and any documents needed to support the application.
  4. Compare complete offers. Review repayments, fees, security, term, balloon payment and settlement timing before accepting.

Common questions

What types of business assets can be financed? The provider lists trucks, machinery, construction equipment, agricultural equipment, medical equipment, office fitouts, technology and manufacturing equipment. It says both new and used equipment typically qualifies.

How fast can approval happen? The source page says pre approval is often available within 24 hours for standard applications, and full approval typically takes 2 to 5 business days depending on complexity and documentation completeness.

Is extra security always needed for equipment finance? The provider says the equipment itself serves as security for most equipment finance, and that additional security is rarely required for standard applications. Unsecured options are also described as available.

Should tax decide the loan structure? Tax treatment is a key question, but it should not be guessed. The published FAQ says the best chattel mortgage or finance lease choice depends on the specific tax situation and recommends speaking with an accountant.
