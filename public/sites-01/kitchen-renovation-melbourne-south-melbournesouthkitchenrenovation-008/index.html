Kitchen Renovation Melbourne South Explained

The Melbourne South Kitchen Renovation description starts with practical failures: drawers that no longer work, doors that will not stay shut, limited bench space or a layout that does not suit the household. Record those problems before selecting cabinets, because they determine storage, circulation and working areas.

Homes across Melbourne's south east and outer east differ in age, proportions and block size. A kitchen in Wantirna South, Glen Waverley or Blackburn South may therefore need a different layout response from another room. Examine the walls, services, access and usable floor area rather than treating suburb as the design brief.

Ask to see where the pantry, appliances, sink, cooktop, splashback and drawers will sit. The published service description refers to 3D designs, allowing the arrangement to be reviewed before construction. A laundry or vanity can also be considered if it belongs in the same project.

Plan the work behind the cabinets

A full renovation may include the layout, cabinetry, benchtops, plumbing, gas, electrical work, tiling and painting. The quote should identify what is being moved, replaced or retained, especially where the sink, cooktop or other services change position.

Removing old cabinets exposes walls and pipework more clearly. In an older home, that inspection can reveal work that was not visible at the first discussion. A written scope cannot remove every unknown, but it should describe the agreed work and state how allowances or changes will be handled.

  • Plumbing and electrical rough-in occur before walls close and cabinets are fitted.
  • Cabinetry and benchtops must align with the planned sink, cooktop and splashback.
  • Tiling and painting follow the earlier installation stages.

Victoria's published guidance says regulated plumbing and electrical work should be completed by licensed trades who issue certificates. The quote should name the responsible person for each trade.

Match cabinetry and surfaces to the room

Flat pack cabinets can suit a more standard cabinet approach where the existing room and budget favour simplicity. Custom joinery is useful when storage must be built around the room, such as a tall pantry wall, butler's pantry or unusual dimensions.

Natural stone should be considered alongside the layout, not after it. The sink, cooktop and splashback need to be planned with the benchtop and cabinetry. Ask which material, edge details, cut-outs and installation work are included in the written price.

In a small kitchen, extra cabinets can reduce useful preparation space or movement. Measure what is genuinely missing, then compare drawers, pantry storage, taller units and a revised work area against the way the household cooks.

Use the quote and schedule to compare options

Cost depends on kitchen size, materials and whether plumbing or electrical positions move. Flat pack sits at the lower end of the range described by the provider, while custom joinery with natural stone sits at the higher end. Compare cabinets, handles, benchtops, appliances, removal, trade work, tiling, painting and allowances rather than a single headline figure.

The schedule should show removal, rough-in work, cabinet delivery, installation and the expected period without a working kitchen. Keeping the existing layout can finish sooner than moving plumbing or adding walls. Custom cabinets can add lead time before installation, so ask when measurements are final and when manufacturing starts.

  • Which work is included or excluded?
  • Who coordinates the cabinet maker, plumber, electrician and tiler?
  • What allowance applies to hidden issues in an older home?
  • Which warranties cover products and installation?

Common questions

What can a full kitchen renovation include? It can include layout, cabinets, benchtops, plumbing, gas, electrical work, tiling and painting. The exact scope should be written before work starts.

Does keeping the existing layout affect timing? The published guidance says a kitchen that keeps its layout can finish sooner than one with moved plumbing or new walls. Custom cabinets can also add lead time.

Are flat pack cabinets suitable for every kitchen? No. Flat pack can suit a simpler cabinet approach, while custom joinery may be better where storage must follow the room's dimensions.

What should a quote show? It should identify cabinets, benchtops, trades, hidden-issue allowances, responsibility for regulated work and the expected schedule.

What warranty questions should homeowners ask? Ask which manufacturer warranties apply to cabinets, benchtops and appliances, and what workmanship warranty covers installation. Australian Consumer Law guarantees also apply to fixed items such as stoves, sinks, tapware and extractor fans.
