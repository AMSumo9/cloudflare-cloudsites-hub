Door type changes the repair conversation

The page separates roller doors, sectional doors, springs, motors and general diagnostics because each points to a different fault path. A roller door may involve a curtain, drum, cable, track or motor issue. A sectional or panel-lift door may involve panels, hinges, springs or tracks. A garage door spring replacement is described as torsion or extension spring replacement for broken or fatigued springs, with safety-critical handling and a fixed-price quote before work.

That distinction is useful when writing the enquiry. Instead of saying the door is broken, describe what the door is doing: stuck, noisy, off-track, failing to open, moving unevenly, or not responding to the remote. Include the suburb or postcode because the published process asks for it, then let the repairer diagnose the fault.
