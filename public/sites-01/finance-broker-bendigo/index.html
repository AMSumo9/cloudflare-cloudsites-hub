Documents And Questions To Prepare

Comparing Structures Without Guesswork

The source page gives a useful distinction between chattel mortgage and finance lease. With a chattel mortgage, it says the borrower owns the asset from day one, can claim GST upfront and may benefit from depreciation deductions. With a finance lease, it says the lender owns the asset while the business leases it, GST is charged on repayments, and the asset can be returned or purchased at the end.

Those statements should be treated as a starting point for questions, not personal tax advice. A Bendigo business comparing structures should ask the broker and accountant how ownership, GST timing, residual value, balloon payments and cash flow interact. The right answer can differ between a trade vehicle, a medical fitout, a harvester, a construction machine or general technology equipment.

Interest-only loans, loan-to-value ratio and lenders mortgage insurance are common finance concepts, but they should only enter the conversation when the specific product requires them. For this provider's published page, the dominant categories are commercial assets, business loans and working capital rather than a detailed home loan advice page.
