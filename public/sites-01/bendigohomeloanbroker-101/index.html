Borrowers in Kangaroo Flat, Strathdale, and Golden Square often face a choice between approaching their existing bank or seeking a home loan broker Bendigo residents trust. A direct approach limits you to that lender's products and servicing criteria. Conversely, a broker compares a wide panel, assessing rates, features, and policies side by side. For those looking to refinance, the comparison extends to your current lender's retention offer. A broker can benchmark this against the market to determine if switching genuinely saves money or if your current bank can match the competition.

Structuring Investment and Refinance Loans

Investment lending requires distinct considerations compared to owner-occupier loans. Investors in Epsom and Eaglehawk must decide between interest-only and principal and interest repayments. An interest-only period minimises monthly outgoings, potentially improving cash flow, though principal repayments are eventually required. Brokers assess these options alongside tax implications and lender policy, which varies significantly regarding acceptable debt-to-income ratios for investors.

Refinancing involves checking exit fees from your current loan and establishing whether equity can be accessed for renovations or further investment. A home loan broker Bendigo investors use can model these scenarios to ensure the new loan structure aligns with long-term wealth goals rather than just securing a lower introductory rate.

First Home Buyer Pathways and Schemes

Entering the property market in Central Victoria often involves navigating state and federal incentives. The Victorian First Home Owner Grant provides a financial boost for eligible new home purchases, with thresholds managed by the State Revenue Office. Federally, the First Home Guarantee allows eligible buyers to purchase with a deposit as low as 5 per cent without paying lenders mortgage insurance, subject to price caps that change annually.

A broker helps determine your eligibility for these schemes and how they interact. For example, combining a small deposit with a guarantee requires a lender that participates in the scheme, and not all do. Furthermore, a larger deposit than the minimum still reduces overall interest costs, so brokers often advise saving beyond the 5 per cent floor where possible.

Broker Accreditation and Consumer Protections

Choosing a finance professional involves verifying their credentials. Reputable brokers in Australia hold an Australian Credit Licence or operate as Credit Representatives under a licensee. Industry accreditation through bodies like the Mortgage and Finance Association of Australia (MFAA) indicates adherence to professional standards.

Regulations provide strong consumer safeguards. Since January 2021, brokers must act in the best interests of the consumer when providing credit assistance. Additionally, membership with the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA) ensures access to free external dispute resolution if a dispute arises that cannot be resolved directly with the broker or lender.

Common questions

Do brokers charge a fee for their service? Most residential mortgage brokers in Australia do not charge borrowers a fee for their service. They are typically paid commission by the lender, though any fees or exceptions must be disclosed upfront as per regulatory requirements.

How long is pre-approval valid for? Pre-approval, often called conditional approval, is typically valid for around 90 days. This timeframe depends on the specific lender and your financial circumstances remaining stable during the period.

Can a broker help with bad credit history? While this guide focuses on standard residential pathways, brokers generally have access to specialist lenders who may consider borrowers with complex financial histories. It is important to disclose your full situation during the initial enquiry.
