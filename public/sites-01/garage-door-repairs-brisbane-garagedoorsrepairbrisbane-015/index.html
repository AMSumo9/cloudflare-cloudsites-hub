Repair Or Replacement

The published guide separates standard servicing, spring replacement, cable replacement, roller replacement, motor replacement and new sectional door installation. Those are different decisions, so compare the written scope rather than only the headline price.

A door that moves poorly may lead to servicing, rollers, cables or spring assessment. A powered door that does not respond may need opener, remote, keypad or motor diagnosis. Damaged sections may raise a panel replacement question before a new door quote is considered.

Ask what is being replaced, what is being adjusted, and whether the technician gives a written fixed-price quote before work starts.

Powered Openers

Powered openers add one extra quote check. The source page states that electrical work on powered openers is carried out by a licensed electrician under the Queensland Electrical Safety Act 2002.

For opener or motor replacement, the published guide range is $600 to $1,100. Before approving that work, ask whether the diagnosis points to the motor unit, remote programming, keypad programming, power supply or door hardware. The quote should identify the opener issue and the door work, if any, that sits beside it.
