Gas Fitting Melton Explained

Plumber Melton describes gas work as including leak checks and new cooktop connections. Separate an urgent situation from a repair enquiry before discussing the job. If the smell is strong or anyone feels unwell, get everyone outside without touching switches and call 000 from a safe distance.

For a faint smell, close the appliance gas tap, open windows and keep flames away. Turn the gas off at the meter if it is safe to reach. If the smell remains, call the emergency number on your gas bill. Record the appliance and room involved so the later enquiry is specific.

A smell near a cooktop needs tracing rather than guesswork. Note whether it began after use, whether it is constant and whether other gas appliances are affected.

Define the work at the property

The job may involve checking for a leak, tracing a smell or making a new cooktop connection. Explain the appliance, its location and any access around cabinetry. This helps establish whether the visit concerns diagnosis, repair or installation.

  • For a suspected leak, identify where the smell is strongest and whether it changes during appliance use.
  • For a cooktop connection, provide the appliance type and describe access around the cabinetry.
  • For hot water symptoms, state whether the system is gas, electric or solar and describe what has changed.

The published service information lists gas hot water alongside electric and solar systems. A cold shower therefore does not automatically identify the required trade. The cause may be an ignition fault, failed element, leaking tank or ageing system, so the system type and symptoms matter.

Check licensing and certificates

Victoria requires plumbing work to be carried out by a plumber with a current licence or registration, or by a supervised apprentice. Gas work should be discussed with a licensed gasfitter, with the proposed task and responsibility clear before work begins.

Some jobs require a compliance certificate. The published guidance says the licensed plumber must provide it within five days of finishing when one is required. Ask before approval whether the proposed gas work falls into that category and how the certificate will be supplied.

Keep the written price, work description and any certificate with the appliance records. The website also states that Australian plumbing services carry automatic consumer guarantees, including due care and skill. Any warranty on the job is additional to those protections.

When gas work forms part of a wider renovation, ASIC Moneysmart renovating guidance can provide broader project context alongside trade-specific advice.

Compare quotes by scope, not just price

A useful quote describes the reported fault, proposed action and conditions that could change the scope. Access, parts and testing may differ between a simple connection and a fault that needs tracing. The written price before work starts gives the homeowner a clear decision point.

  1. Describe the symptom, when it began and which appliance is involved.
  2. Ask whether the visit is for diagnosis, repair, replacement or a new connection.
  3. Confirm whether parts, testing and any certificate are included.
  4. Ask what happens if inspection identifies a different cause.

Include the suburb and access details in the enquiry. The business names Melton, Kurunjang, Melton South, Aintree and Fraser Rise among the areas it works around. A location alone does not define the job, but it helps place the request before a price is considered.

Who this guide applies to

This guide is for homeowners and occupants dealing with a suspected gas issue, cooktop connection, gas hot water symptom or gas work during renovation. It is also useful when preparing the details for a first enquiry.

It is not a substitute for emergency services or a licensed inspection. Strong smells or illness require the emergency response above. A faint smell that remains after the initial steps should be reported through the emergency number on the gas bill.

For a related water-heating question, the Melton hot water repairs guide offers a separate starting point. Keep the scopes distinct because a hot water fault may involve gas, electric or solar equipment.

  1. Record the symptom. Write down the appliance, room, timing and whether the smell or fault changes during use.
  2. Make the area safe. Follow the appropriate strong-smell or faint-smell response before making an enquiry.
  3. Request a defined scope. Ask whether the proposed work is diagnosis, repair, replacement or a new connection.
  4. Confirm the paperwork. Ask whether a compliance certificate is required and how it will be provided.

Common questions

Who can carry out gas work in Victoria? Gas work should be discussed with a licensed gasfitter. Victorian plumbing work must be done by a plumber with a current licence or registration, or by a supervised apprentice.

What should I do if I smell gas strongly? Get everyone outside without touching switches and call 000 from a safe distance, particularly if anyone feels unwell.

What should I do if the smell is faint? Close the appliance gas tap, open windows, keep flames away and turn off the meter only if safe. If the smell remains, call the emergency number on your gas bill.

Does every cold shower need a gasfitter? No. A cold shower may involve an ignition fault, failed element, leaking tank or ageing system. State the system type and symptoms so the appropriate scope can be considered.

When should I ask about a compliance certificate? Ask before work begins whether the job requires one. When required, the licensed plumber must provide it within five days of finishing.
