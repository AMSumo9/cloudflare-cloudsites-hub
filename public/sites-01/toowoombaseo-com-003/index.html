That makes the first review worth judging as a working document. A sensible first conversation also distinguishes between visibility for the main service and visibility for every supporting service. That prevents the scope from becoming too broad before the strongest commercial opportunity is identified.

That breadth is relevant because stronger visibility only matters if the visitor can quickly understand the offer and take the next step.

If the advice focuses on traffic but says little about page clarity, mobile use or the path to enquiry, part of the problem may be missing. For some businesses, the right first move may be a clearer homepage, sharper service copy or a better page structure rather than a full rebuild. A good review should make that distinction obvious.

A grounded answer should come back to understandable page content, clear business information and service pages that match how people search.
