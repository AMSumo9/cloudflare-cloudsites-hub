Common questions

What deposit do first home buyers need in Brisbane? Most lenders require a minimum deposit of 5 to 20 percent of the property value. Deposits under 20 percent typically attract Lenders Mortgage Insurance, which adds to the upfront cost. Some government schemes may help bridge deposit gaps for eligible buyers. Your broker can explain specific requirements based on your circumstances.

How do I know if I qualify for the First Home Owner Grant? Eligibility depends on factors including your age, property value, and whether the home is new or established. In Queensland, grants are generally available for new homes or substantially renovated properties valued under certain thresholds.
