Documents and Approval Checks

The page states that pre-approval is often available within 24 hours for standard applications, while full approval typically takes 2 to 5 business days depending on complexity and documentation completeness. Treat those timings as conditional because the faster path depends on the asset, borrower profile and whether requested documents are ready.

Before asking for quotes, prepare the purchase invoice or asset description, business identification, recent financial information, the finance amount sought and any preferred term or balloon structure. For unsecured or working capital requests, expect the conversation to focus more heavily on cash flow, purpose and repayment capacity because there may not be a specific asset supporting the facility.

Choosing a Structure

The published page names chattel mortgage, finance lease and hire purchase as equipment finance options. It also says a chattel mortgage means the borrower owns the asset from day one, can claim GST upfront and may benefit from depreciation deductions. Under a finance lease, the lender owns the asset while the borrower leases it, and GST is charged on repayments rather than upfront.

Those tax references should be checked with an accountant before choosing a structure. A broker can compare lender options, but the better questions are how the structure affects cash flow, ownership, end-of-term choices and the way the business reports the asset. ASIC Moneysmart encourages borrowers to compare costs and features before committing to a loan.
