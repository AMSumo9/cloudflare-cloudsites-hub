What To Prepare Before A Review

A stronger review starts with the facts that narrow lender fit. The published page points borrowers toward a borrowing power calculator, planning checklist and resource guides before they enquire. It also asks for suburb or postcode, the problem being solved and enough context to shape the next step.

For a purchase, prepare the deposit position, property type, expected repayment comfort and whether pre approval is needed before auction, offer or a serious property search. For a refinance, bring current loan details, fixed rate expiry if relevant, the reason for switching and whether equity access is part of the plan. For an investor, prepare the rental assumption being used and whether the property is already held or still being shortlisted.

Self employed borrowers should summarise the business structure and available income records before the lender shortlist is discussed. That can help separate realistic options from lenders that may not fit the file.

How To Compare Advice

A broker comparison should go beyond an advertised rate. Ask why a proposed path suits the file, what assumptions were used and which details still need checking.

If LMI, a guarantee or a low deposit pathway is being considered, ask how the structure affects repayments and lender choice. If refinancing, ask whether switching value remains after costs and whether equity access changes the structure. If pre approval is the goal, ask what could change once a property is chosen.

Scheme and policy assumptions should be checked at the time of review. The page says first home and grant guidance is checked against Revenue NSW, Housing Australia and APRA settings, so borrowers should ask for the current rule rather than relying on a remembered threshold.

  1. Name the blocker. Write down whether the main issue is deposit, pre approval, refinance value, investment structure or self employed documentation.

  3. Ask for policy checks. Request current checks on grants, duty, LMI, guarantor options and lender rules where they are relevant to the scenario.
  4. Compare the trade off. Judge each loan path by repayments, document fit, flexibility and switching value, not by the advertised rate alone.

Common questions

Is a low deposit pathway automatically suitable? No. The published page describes 5-10% deposit pathways, guarantor structures, LMI trade offs and lender policy comparison, so suitability depends on the borrower file and the lender rules being tested.

What should a first home buyer ask first? Ask how deposit size, grant and duty settings, LMI and repayment comfort interact. The page treats those as linked checks rather than separate boxes.

When is a refinance review useful? It is useful when a borrower wants to test rate review, fixed rate expiry, equity access or switching value before moving lenders. The review should compare the value of changing against the cost and structure of the current loan.

Can self employed borrowers apply for home loans? Yes. The published FAQ says lender choice and document quality matter more, with tax returns, BAS history, entity structure and purchase timing all able to affect which lenders are realistic.
