This guide applies to Ipswich borrowers who are still trying to make the loan decision practical rather than theoretical. Ipswich Timing Questions Before You Offer

  1. Define the scenario. State whether the loan is for a first purchase, refinance, investment, construction, upgrade or pre-approval.
  2. Gather the documents. Prepare income, savings, current loan, property and contract information before asking for lender comparisons.

  4. Compare fit. Review repayments, features, fees, timing and lender policy instead of treating the headline rate as the full answer.
