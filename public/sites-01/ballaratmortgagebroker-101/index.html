Comparing First Home Buyer Support

First home buyers in regional Victoria have several government-backed schemes to consider when comparing their entry strategy. The Regional First Home Buyer Guarantee allows eligible buyers to purchase a home with a deposit as low as 5%, avoiding Lenders Mortgage Insurance. This differs from the standard First Home Guarantee, which also offers a 5% deposit pathway but is subject to national place caps. Additionally, the Victorian Homebuyer Fund provides a shared equity option where the state government contributes to the deposit. When weighing these options, it is essential to check current eligibility thresholds and place caps on the State Revenue Office website or consult an accredited broker who can explain the nuances of each scheme.

Refinancing and Switching Costs

Refinancing is not just about securing a lower advertised rate. A thorough comparison must account for the total cost of switching. Lenders may charge discharge fees to exit your current loan and establishment or valuation fees for the new one. A broker can model these figures to ensure the savings from a new rate outweigh the upfront costs. Borrowers in Lake Wendouree or Alfredton looking to refinance should also review if their current loan features, such as an offset account or free extra repayments, are retained in the new product. Sometimes, a slightly higher rate with better features can offer greater value than a basic loan with a rock-bottom rate.

The Broker versus Bank Choice

One of the most significant comparisons a borrower makes is between using a broker or going directly to a bank. A bank manager can only offer products from their specific institution, limiting your view to one suite of loans. In contrast, a mortgage broker compares a panel of lenders on your behalf. Since 1 January 2021, brokers are bound by the statutory best interests duty, meaning they must recommend a loan that is in your best interests, not just the lender's. For those comparing complex financial scenarios, a broker provides access to a wider market and does the legwork of comparing application requirements and turnaround times across different institutions.

Common questions

How does a comparison rate differ from an advertised rate? The comparison rate includes the interest rate plus most fees and charges associated with the loan, providing a clearer view of the total cost than the headline rate alone.

Is it worth refinancing for a small rate drop? It depends on the switching costs. A broker can model discharge fees and new loan charges to determine if the long-term savings justify the expense of moving lenders.

Can I use the Regional First Home Buyer Guarantee for an existing home? Yes, the guarantee can apply to both new and established homes in regional areas, provided you meet the eligibility criteria and the property value caps.
